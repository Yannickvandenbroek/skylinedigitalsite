Frequently asked questions

About pricing

Are these fixed prices?

They're guide prices as a starting point. After a short call you'll get a transparent, tailored quote with no surprises afterwards.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, for larger projects we work in instalments — for example at kick-off, halfway and on delivery.

Is maintenance included in the price?

Every website package (Horizon, Skyline and Panorama) includes 1 month of aftercare. After that you can take an affordable maintenance subscription.

What if I want several services?

Then we put together a tailored Premium package — often better value than adding up separate services.
